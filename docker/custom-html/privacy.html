Fibo Planner respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

Fibo Planner does not require an account and does not intentionally collect information such as your name, email address, or telephone number.

The Fibo Planner visitor counter collects limited technical information about requests to the service. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser and HTTP request headers
  • Requested URL and query parameters
  • HTTP method
  • Date and time of the request

This information is recorded by the web-counter service and stored using Firebase/Google Cloud services. It is used for visitor counting, statistics, security, and operation of the service.

Cookies and Tracking

Fibo Planner does not use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

The visitor counter is used for basic usage statistics and is not intended to identify individual users.

Third-Party Services

Fibo Planner uses Firebase and Google Cloud infrastructure to operate the visitor counter and related services. These providers may process technical information as necessary to provide their services.

The default UI loads HTMX and the HTMX WebSocket extension from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Your browser requests those scripts directly, so the CDN can see your IP address and request metadata.

Data Retention

Visitor-counter records may be retained for as long as they are needed for the operation and statistical purposes of the service.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Fibo Planner project through its GitHub repository.
